## Q1
type: choice
topic: Present Perfect
q: She ___ here since 2020.
options: [lives, has lived, is living, lived]
answer: has lived
explain: "Since + a point in time" and the action still continues → Present Perfect (or Present Perfect Continuous).

## Q2
type: choice
topic: Past Continuous
q: I ___ dinner when the phone rang.
options: [cooked, was cooking, have cooked, had cooked]
answer: was cooking
explain: A longer action in progress (Past Continuous) interrupted by a short one (Past Simple: rang).

## Q3
type: choice
topic: Future
q: Look at those clouds! It ___ rain.
options: [will, is going to, rains, is raining]
answer: is going to
explain: A prediction based on present evidence (the clouds) → be going to.

## Q4
type: gap
topic: Past Perfect
q: By the time we arrived, the film ___ (already / start).
answer: had already started | had started
explain: One past action happened before another past action (arrived) → Past Perfect: had + V3.

## Q5
type: gap
topic: Present Simple
q: Water ___ (boil) at 100 degrees Celsius.
answer: boils
explain: A general fact / scientific truth → Present Simple. He/she/it → add -s/-es.

## Q6
type: gap
topic: Present Continuous
q: Be quiet, please — the baby ___ (sleep).
answer: is sleeping | 's sleeping
explain: An action happening right now → Present Continuous: am/is/are + V-ing.

## Q7
type: transform
topic: Passive
q: "Rewrite in the Passive: They are building a bridge."
answer: A bridge is being built. | A bridge is being built by them.
explain: Present Continuous passive = am/is/are + being + V3.

## Q8
type: transform
topic: Passive
q: Rewrite in the Passive: Someone stole my bike last night.
answer: My bike was stolen last night.
explain: Past Simple passive = was/were + V3. We omit "by someone" because the doer is unknown.
hint: Start with "My bike…"

## Q9
type: transform
topic: Present Perfect
q: Rewrite using for: I started learning English two years ago. (I have…)
answer: I have been learning English for two years. | I have learned English for two years. | I've been learning English for two years. | I have learnt English for two years.
explain: A past start that continues until now → Present Perfect (Continuous) + for + period.

## Q10
type: error
topic: Present Perfect
q: I am knowing him for ten years.
answer: I have known him for ten years. | I've known him for ten years.
explain: "Know" is a stative verb (no Continuous), and "for ten years" up to now needs Present Perfect.

## Q11
type: error
topic: Future
q: I will call you when I will arrive.
answer: I will call you when I arrive. | I'll call you when I arrive.
explain: After when / if / as soon as / before / after we use Present Simple for the future, not will.

## Q12
type: order
topic: Present Perfect
q: Put the words in the correct order.
chunks: [Have, you, ever, been, to London?]
explain: Present Perfect question: Have + subject + ever + V3. "Been to" = visited and come back.

## Q13
type: order
topic: Passive
chunks: [The letters, will, be, sent, tomorrow]
explain: Future Simple passive = will + be + V3.

## Q14
type: match
topic: Formulas
q: Match each tense with its formula.
pairs:
  - Present Perfect: have/has + V3
  - Past Continuous: was/were + V-ing
  - Future Perfect: will have + V3
  - Past Perfect: had + V3
  - Present Continuous Passive: am/is/are + being + V3
explain: Perfect = have + V3; Continuous = be + V-ing; Passive = be + V3.

## Q15
type: tf
topic: Present Perfect
q: "\"I have seen him yesterday\" is a correct sentence."
answer: false
explain: With a finished time expression (yesterday) use Past Simple → I saw him yesterday.

## Q16
type: tf
topic: Past Simple
q: The Past Simple of "teach" is "taught".
answer: true
explain: teach → taught → taught (irregular verb).

## R1
type: reading
title: A Day That Changed Everything
passage: |
  When Olena first moved to London in 2019, she didn't know anyone. She had studied English at school
  in Lviv for eleven years, but she had never spoken to a native speaker before she arrived.

  On her first morning, she was walking to the underground station when she realised that she had left
  her phone at home. While she was looking for a map, an old man asked her if she needed help. His name
  was Peter, and he had been working as a taxi driver for almost forty years.

  Today, Olena works as a designer for a small studio. She has lived in London for six years, and she
  and Peter are still friends. Every Sunday they meet for coffee, and next year they are going to visit
  Lviv together. "By then," Olena says, "Peter will have learned at least ten Ukrainian words."
questions:
  - type: choice
    topic: Past Perfect
    q: Before she arrived in London, Olena…
    options: [had spoken to many native speakers, had never spoken to a native speaker, was speaking with Peter, has spoken to a native speaker]
    answer: had never spoken to a native speaker
    explain: The text says "she had never spoken to a native speaker before she arrived" — Past Perfect for the earlier past.
  - type: tf
    topic: Past Continuous
    q: Olena was walking to the station when she noticed she didn't have her phone.
    answer: true
    explain: '"She was walking to the underground station when she realised that she had left her phone at home."'
  - type: tf
    topic: Present Simple
    q: Peter still works as a taxi driver.
    answer: not given
    explain: We only know he had been working as a taxi driver for almost forty years (back in 2019). The text doesn't say what he does now.
  - type: gap
    topic: Present Perfect
    q: Olena ___ in London for six years.
    answer: has lived | has been living | 's lived
    explain: '"She has lived in London for six years" — Present Perfect + for, the action continues now.'
  - type: choice
    topic: Future
    q: '"Peter will have learned ten words" — which tense is it?'
    options: [Future Simple, Future Continuous, Future Perfect, Present Perfect]
    answer: Future Perfect
    explain: will have + V3 = Future Perfect — an action completed before a point in the future ("by then").
