# English Tenses — Cheat Sheet

Every English tense is a combination of a **time** (Past / Present / Future) and an **aspect** (Simple / Continuous / Perfect / Perfect Continuous).

## The 12 tenses at a glance

| Tense | Formula (active) | Example | Typical markers | Українською |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Present Simple | V / V-s | She **works** in Kyiv. | always, usually, every day | регулярні дії, факти |
| Present Continuous | am/is/are + V-ing | She **is working** now. | now, at the moment, today | дія відбувається зараз |
| Present Perfect | have/has + V3 | She **has worked** here since 2020. | already, yet, ever, since, for | результат / досвід до тепер |
| Present Perfect Continuous | have/has been + V-ing | She **has been working** for 3 hours. | for, since, all day | тривала дія до тепер |
| Past Simple | V2 / V-ed | She **worked** yesterday. | yesterday, ago, in 2019, last week | завершена дія в минулому |
| Past Continuous | was/were + V-ing | She **was working** at 5 pm. | at 5 pm yesterday, while, when | процес у минулому |
| Past Perfect | had + V3 | She **had worked** before she left. | by the time, before, after | «минуле до минулого» |
| Past Perfect Continuous | had been + V-ing | She **had been working** for hours. | for, since, before | тривалість до моменту в минулому |
| Future Simple | will + V | She **will work** tomorrow. | tomorrow, next week, I think | рішення, прогноз |
| Future Continuous | will be + V-ing | She **will be working** at 5. | at this time tomorrow | процес у майбутньому |
| Future Perfect | will have + V3 | She **will have worked** by 6. | by Friday, by then | завершиться до моменту |
| Future Perfect Continuous | will have been + V-ing | She **will have been working** for 10 years. | for … by … | тривалість до моменту в майбутньому |

> **V** = base form, **V2** = past simple form, **V3** = past participle (go → went → **gone**).

## Passive voice by tense

Passive = **be** (in the right tense) + **V3**.

| Tense | Active | Passive |
|---|---|---|
| Present Simple | They **build** bridges. | Bridges **are built**. |
| Present Continuous | They **are building** a bridge. | A bridge **is being built**. |
| Present Perfect | They **have built** a bridge. | A bridge **has been built**. |
| Past Simple | They **built** a bridge. | A bridge **was built**. |
| Past Continuous | They **were building** a bridge. | A bridge **was being built**. |
| Past Perfect | They **had built** a bridge. | A bridge **had been built**. |
| Future Simple | They **will build** a bridge. | A bridge **will be built**. |
| Future Perfect | They **will have built** a bridge. | A bridge **will have been built**. |

Perfect Continuous tenses are almost never used in the passive.

## Present Perfect vs Past Simple

- **Present Perfect** — the time is *not finished* or *not important*: *I **have seen** this film.*
- **Past Simple** — a *finished* time is mentioned or clear: *I **saw** this film **last week**.*

```text
since + point in time   → since 2020, since Monday, since I was a child
for   + period of time  → for two years, for a week, for ages
```

## Common mistakes

1. ~~I am living here since 2019.~~ → I **have been living** / **have lived** here since 2019.
2. ~~When I came, he already left.~~ → When I came, he **had already left**.
3. ~~I will call you when I will arrive.~~ → I will call you when I **arrive**. *(no `will` after when / if / as soon as)*
4. ~~Did you ever be in London?~~ → **Have** you ever **been** to London?
